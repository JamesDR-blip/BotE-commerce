# SPRINT 05: Capability Decomposition

## Status
Completed

## Objective
Decompose monolithic quality logic into discrete, reusable, and composable data capabilities.

## Deliverables
- Independent capabilities: CleaningCapability, NormalizationCapability, ExtractionCapability, ValidationCapability, QACapability.
- Domain services: ValidationService, HealthScoreService, DuplicateDetection, FormatValidation, MissingValueValidation.
- Standard capability specification contract (CapabilitySpec).
